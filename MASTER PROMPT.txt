==================================================

1. PRODUCT POSITIONING
   ==================================================

ARSAVAYA PMS is NOT a channel manager.

ARSAVAYA PMS is NOT intended to connect directly to Booking.com, Airbnb, Agoda, Traveloka, Expedia, or other OTAs.

ARSAVAYA PMS is:

“An operational management system for a professional villa management company.”

Its primary responsibilities are:

* reservation operations
* operational calendar
* guest operations
* arrivals and departures
* check-in and check-out
* housekeeping
* inspection
* maintenance
* staff tasks
* internal financial tracking
* owner management
* management agreements
* management fees
* owner statements
* reporting
* analytics
* automation
* integration monitoring

OTA connectivity must be delegated to an existing certified channel manager.

Official connectivity architecture:

OTA
→ Channel Manager
→ ARSAVAYA Connectivity Layer
→ ARSAVAYA PMS

Never implement:

ARSAVAYA
→ Booking.com directly

ARSAVAYA
→ Airbnb directly

ARSAVAYA
→ Agoda directly

unless this entire architectural decision is explicitly changed by the product owner in the future.

3. CURRENT STORAGE RULE
=======================

The current PMS uses a versioned workspace state stored in D1 with optimistic concurrency / compare-and-swap behavior.

Do NOT normalize the entire PMS database during connectivity work.

Do NOT rewrite bookings, units, tasks, payments, blocks, expenses, automation, and other working entities into a new database model merely for architectural aesthetics.

Preserve working behavior first.

Small normalized tables may be introduced for integration infrastructure where appropriate.

A future normalized transactional PMS schema may be considered when real scale requires it, but it is NOT part of the initial channel-manager integration.

14. RECONCILIATION ENGINE
=========================

External synchronization must be idempotent.

The same external reservation received repeatedly must update the same local reservation.

Never create duplicates.

Reconciliation states should conceptually include:

NEW
MATCHED
UPDATED
CANCELLED
CONFLICT
NEEDS_REVIEW

If an external confirmed reservation conflicts with local PMS data:

do NOT silently discard it.

do NOT silently overwrite important operational data.

Record the conflict and surface it to the operator.

==================================================
15. CANCELLATIONS
=================

Cancellation must preserve booking history.

Cancelled reservations must no longer block availability.

Never permanently delete an important historical reservation merely because the provider reports cancellation.
